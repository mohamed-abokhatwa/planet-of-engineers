Common questions

  • Is it free? Yes. No ads and no payments.
  • Can people see my salary? Never individually. Ranges appear only when 5 or more engineers have shared for the same country and discipline.
  • How do I report or block someone? Use the menu on any post.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app: Me → "Delete my account & data". It is deleted immediately.
  • Are the calculators reliable? They are checked against reference values, but they are estimates. Always verify with your local codes and a licensed engineer.
