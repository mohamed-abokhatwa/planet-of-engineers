# Privacy Policy · سياسة الخصوصية

**Planet of Engineers (كوكب المهندسين)**
Effective date: 1 November 2026

Planet of Engineers is a free app for engineers: offline calculators, a question-and-answer community (Site SOS), anonymous salary statistics and a daily challenge. We collect as little as possible, show no ads, sell no data and do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Who can see it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous account ID** (created automatically) | Nobody | To link your posts and settings to your device. We never ask for your email, phone or password. |
| **Username** | Everyone in the app | Shown on your questions, answers and the leaderboard. |
| **Discipline and country** | Everyone in the app | Shown next to your username, and counted (as totals) in the Planet statistics. |
| **Years of experience and role** | Only you | Shown on your profile and engineer card. |
| **Questions, answers and photos you post** | Everyone in the app | So engineers can help each other. Photos are stored at a public web address that only the app shows. |
| **Votes, reports and blocks** | Nobody (only totals) | To rank answers, remove harmful content and hide people you block. They are never told. |
| **Salary entry** (country, discipline, years, monthly gross, currency) | **Nobody, ever, individually** | Only statistics are shown, and only when at least 5 engineers have shared for the same country and discipline. You can update or delete it. |
| **Daily Challenge answers** | Nobody (only weekly points on the leaderboard) | For your streak and the weekly leaderboard. Individual answers are deleted after 30 days; only totals are kept. |
| **Language and time zone** | Nobody | To show the app in your language. |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID or browsing history. Photos are read only when you choose one to attach.

## 2. Contact details are not allowed

To protect everyone from spam and scams, the app blocks phone numbers, emails, usernames of other apps, messaging apps and links in posts.

## 3. Content moderation

Posts are checked automatically on your device and on our server. Every post has a menu to **report** it or **block** its author. A post reported by 3 different engineers is hidden until we review it. We review reports within 24 hours.

## 4. Service providers

| Provider | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosts the database, anonymous sign-in and photo storage. |
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | App distribution (their own privacy policies apply). |

## 5. How long we keep data and how to delete it

- Your profile, posts, photos and salary entry are kept until you delete them.
- **Me → "Delete my account & data"** permanently deletes your profile, posts, photos, votes, reports, blocks, salary entry and challenge history immediately.

## 6. Security

All data travels over encrypted connections. The database uses row-level security, so the app can only read your own private data. Salary entries and votes are never readable by any user.

## 7. Children

Planet of Engineers is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

## 8. Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, delete everything with one tap, or contact us for any other request. Depending on where you live (for example under Saudi Arabia's PDPL, the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA), you may have additional rights, and we will honour them.
