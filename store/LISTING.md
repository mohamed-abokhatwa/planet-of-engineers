## English

**App name (30):** Planet of Engineers
**Subtitle, Apple (30):** Engineering tools & answers

**Description**

The world is engineers… and a few other people.

Planet of Engineers is a free home for engineers everywhere: civil, structural, mechanical, electrical, chemical, architecture, software and more. No ads, no sign-up, and the toolbox works without internet on site.

🧰 TOOLBOX: 47 OFFLINE CALCULATORS
• Structural: beam moment, shear and deflection, column buckling, section properties, rebar spacing
• Civil: concrete and cement bags, rebar weight, blocks, tiles, plaster, paint, excavation and trucks, steel weight, tanks, stairs
• Electrical: power and current, voltage drop and cable size, Ohm's law, power factor correction, motor current, transformer and fault level, lighting, UPS backup, solar PV
• Mechanical and HVAC: pipe pressure drop, pump power, cooling load, duct and pipe sizing, heat transfer, pulleys, bolt torque, hydrostatic pressure, thermal expansion
• Process, surveying and maths: dilution, ideal gas, distance and bearing, height by angle, DMS, interpolation and more
• Scientific calculator, 17-category unit converter, reference tables (rebar, IPE, concrete grades, bolts, Sch 40 pipes, AWG, densities)
• Search, favourites, remembered inputs, copy and share results
Every formula has been checked against handbook values and an independent second implementation.

🆘 SITE SOS
Stuck on site? Ask engineers around the planet, attach a photo, and vote on the best answers.

🧭 SALARY COMPASS
See what engineers really earn by country, discipline and experience. Share yours anonymously; figures appear only when at least 5 engineers have shared.

⚡ DAILY CHALLENGE
One engineering question a day. Keep your streak and climb the leaderboard.

🎨 Dark or light theme, English and Arabic.

Results are estimates. Always check against your local codes and a licensed engineer.

## Apple: App Privacy

- **Tracking:** No.
- **Data linked to the user** (the anonymous account), used for App Functionality:
  - User Content: Photos or Videos; Other User Content
  - Identifiers: User ID
  - Financial Info: Other Financial Info (salary, optional)
  - Other Data: profile fields (username, country, discipline, experience)
- **Data not linked to the user:** Diagnostics: Crash Data (once crash reporting is switched on).

## Age rating

- Apple: answer "Yes" to user-generated content and unmoderated communication **with** moderation (report, block, filters). Everything else "None". Expect 13+ on Apple's current scale.
- Google (IARC): users can interact and share content: Yes. Shares location: No. Digital purchases: No. Expect Teen / 12+.

## Notes for Apple review

> No login is needed: the app creates an anonymous account automatically on first launch.
> User-generated content (Guideline 1.2): users accept the terms with zero tolerance for abuse during onboarding; posts are filtered automatically on device and on the server (abuse, contact details and phone numbers are blocked); every post has Report and Block; a post reported by 3 users is hidden automatically; reports are reviewed daily. Support email is in the app.
> Account deletion (5.1.1(v)): Me → Delete my account & data.
> Photos are only accessed when the user chooses to attach one.
> Engineering results are labelled as estimates on every calculator and in the Terms.
